description: Work hands-on with data and learn how to organize, explore, and visualize it effectively. You’ll also practice thinking critically about what your data can—and cannot—tell you.
learning_objectives:
  - "**Distinguish** common types and stages of research data"
  - "**Apply** good practices for structuring and organizing data"
  - "**Analyze** and **visualize** a small dataset using spreadsheet tools"
  - "**Compare** spreadsheet- and code-based approaches to data analysis, including their implications for transparency, reproducibility, and reuse"
  - "**Evaluate** the limitations of a dataset and the conclusions that can reasonably be drawn from it"
